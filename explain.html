Questions here:


article - The content's suitable for article because the information came from a blog post.


aside - It keeps the content off to the side. I used it to emphasize the BBQ Chicken Pizza.


details - Opening and closing it shows and hides the information in the element. I used it to show the answer to the previously asked question.


figcaption - It explains what the figure is supposed to show or represent. I used it to name exactly what toppings are on the pizza shown.


figure - They're grouped together to show that they are related. I used this to show an example of one of the pizzas on the list.


footer - The footer contains secondary information and navigation links. I decided to put the link to this page inside of the footer tag.


header - It contains the heading at the very top that introduces the topic of the page. It's at the very top of the body tag.


main - It's the primary content of the page since it's the part of the site that the title refers to. I put the list inside of it.


nav - The links are here because they take you to either of the two pages. I used the nav tag to link this and the previous page together.


section - Since it's a list, all the contents have to be related. I used this tag for the list of weird pizzas.


summary - The role this element plays is to be a clickable header that shows and hides the details. I used it to set up a question that would be answered when clicked.
